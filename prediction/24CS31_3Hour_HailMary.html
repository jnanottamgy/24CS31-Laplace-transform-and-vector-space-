8Solve ODEs and simultaneous ODEs by Laplace transforms 07 marks · Q3d or Q4d — 4/4 papers · the item I wrongly cut
(a) Simultaneous — dxdt − 2y = cos 2t;  dydt + 2x = sin 2t,  x(0)=1, y(0)=0.
(b) Single ODE — y″ + 4y′ + 3y = e−t (set twice, with different initial conditions).
(c) Circuit — Ldidt + Ri = Ee−at, i(0)=0  →  i = ER − aL(e−at − e−Rt/L).
Appears alongside: the Heaviside question (step 5) in the same question — 4/4 papers.
One method for all three. Transform every term with L{y′} = sY − y(0) and L{y″} = s2Y − sy(0) − y′(0). For a single ODE, solve for Y(s) and invert by partial fractions. For a system, you get two linear equations in X(s) and Y(s) — solve them as simultaneous algebra, then invert each. The initial conditions do the work; write them in at the transform step and the rest is algebra.

12Rotation-operator proof 07 marks · Q5c — Apr 23 and Mar 24 · the item I wrongly cut

Appears alongside: the span/basis and prove-linear questions — this is the part that turns Q5 into a full 20.
Pure bookwork — twelve minutes, no calculus, 7 marks. Write x in polar form: x = (rcos φ, rsin φ). Multiply out Ax and you get (rcos θcos φ − rsin θsin φ,  rsin θcos φ + rcos θsin φ).
