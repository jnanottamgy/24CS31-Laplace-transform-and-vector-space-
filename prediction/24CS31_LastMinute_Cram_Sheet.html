IF YOU HAVE 20 SPARE MINUTES — the one hole in your prep. PCA is named in your syllabus and in CO5, and has never been asked in four papers. If it comes, it's 5 marks in Unit V, most likely next to the SVD. All you need: centre the data (subtract the column means), form the covariance matrix C = 1n−1XTX, find its eigenvalues and eigenvectors, take the eigenvector of the largest eigenvalue as the first principal component, and report λ1/Σλi as the variance it explains. Conic sections is the same gap — diagonalise the quadratic form; both eigenvalues the same sign → ellipse, opposite signs → hyperbola.
